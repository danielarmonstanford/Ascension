Why Ascension

Most retreats hand you a schedule and call it transformation. Ascension works the other way. We bring together practitioners, places and practices across the body's five senses, and you move through them differently than the person beside you. Nobody is prescribed a path here. You find yours.

The Framework

Five senses. One presence.

Sight, sound, touch, taste and scent are the doors in. Presence is what waits on the other side of all five at once. Hold the button below and feel what that means.

Sight

Light, landscape, and the architecture of a place built to be looked at slowly.

Sound

Gong, bowl, and voice, used for restoration rather than performance.

Touch

Bodywork and fascial release, the physical release of what the body has been holding.

Taste

Plant-based, ceremonial meals built around the rhythm of the day, not around a menu.

Scent

The quiet sense. Rarely designed for on purpose, and one of the first things you'll notice.
