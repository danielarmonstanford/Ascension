# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript deployed through Vercel. The repository is intended to use GitHub as its source and deployment pipeline.

## Users

The primary visitor is a prospective ASCENSION guest deciding whether the experience is desirable, credible, appropriate for them, and worth reserving.

The guest journey must prioritize emotional desire, immediate comprehension, trust, program understanding, and then reservation or application.

Practitioners and facilitators, venue partners, sponsors, and other collaborators are important secondary audiences. Their pathways must remain clearly discoverable without competing with the guest journey.

## Product Purpose

ASCENSION SENSES presents and converts interest in a curated, modular experiential wellness and travel program. The website must help guests understand what the experience is, feel its emotional and sensory character, trust its claims, evaluate the available participation formats, and reserve or apply with confidence.

Success means the independent ASCENSION identity is understood quickly, verified program information is easy to evaluate, secondary collaborators can find their route, and qualified prospective guests reach the existing reservation or application infrastructure.

## Positioning

ASCENSION is an independent travelling series of curated experiences across movement, touch, sound, taste, scent, sight, place, and personal wellbeing. It is not a conventional resort package and does not bundle accommodation by default. Guests choose the participation format and normally select and book their own stay while ASCENSION curates the experiential program.

- The original Da Nang yoga-wheel photograph is the primary visual source of truth.

## Product Principles

1. Lead the guest from desire to comprehension to trust to program understanding to action.
2. Make the sensory framework tangible without obscuring practical decision-making.
3. Distill aggressively: one dominant guest journey, with secondary collaborator routes kept clear but subordinate.
4. Treat every commercial, medical, partnership, and program claim as evidence-bound.
5. Build an independent ASCENSION identity while retaining MODUS / SÉANCE and Stanford Emporium only as appropriate provenance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must prioritize legibility, semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, appropriate contrast in both DAY and DUSK, touch-sized controls, reduced-motion alternatives, and clear language. Sensory storytelling must never make core program or reservation information inaccessible.
