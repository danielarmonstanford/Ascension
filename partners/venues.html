How It Works

We curate the program.
You hold the space.

ASCENSION operates on a passport model — attendees pay for the curated program directly; accommodation is booked independently by each guest. Venue partners who provide group rates and a booking code receive commission per room-night confirmed through that code.

There is no attrition clause and no guaranteed block. This is an endorsement relationship: we recommend your property to the ASCENSION cohort and promote your rate. In exchange, verified bookings earn commission.

Booking Code System

Tracking &
attribution.

Each venue partner receives a unique booking code (e.g. ASCENSION-DN27). Guests are directed to quote this code when booking directly with your property. Commission is paid quarterly on verified room-night conversions.

  • Code format: Agreed on LOI signature — unique per property
  • Tracking: Venue confirms attributed bookings monthly to daniel@stanfordemporium.com
  • Commission timing: Paid 30 days after the retreat closes (by February 25, 2027)
  • Minimum threshold: No minimum — commission is paid on any confirmed attributed room-nights
  • Currency: USD, via wire or Wise (agreed on LOI)
No-Attrition Terms

No blocks.
No guarantees.

We don't hold room blocks and carry no liability for attrition. This is a promotional partnership: we endorse your property to the ASCENSION cohort and promote your rate. In exchange, verified bookings earn commission.

  • No minimum commitment: We do not guarantee a minimum number of room-nights
  • No cancellation penalty: Either party may withdraw from the partnership up to 90 days before the retreat (October 14, 2026) with no financial obligation
  • Guest relationship: Guest contracts are directly with your property — we carry no liability for individual booking disputes
  • Rate parity: Rates offered through ASCENSION should be at parity with or better than rates available through OTAs at time of enquiry
Group Enquiries

For groups of 3 or more rooms, we can facilitate a direct group enquiry to the venue on behalf of the cohort — typically 8–12% off published rates, and often with shared transfer arrangements.

To co-create a group enquiry or discuss bespoke venue terms: daniel@stanfordemporium.com

Please include your property name, January 2027 availability window, and group rate availability. We will cross-reference with confirmed guest preferences.
